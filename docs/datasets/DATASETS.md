## How each golden set is built

- **FinanceBench:** all 150 questions. An answer of four words or fewer that contains a digit is `numeric`; otherwise `free_text`. Evidence pages are converted from 0-based to 1-based.
- **PubMedQA:** a stratified sample of 200 from the 1,000 expert-labelled questions (seed 42), keeping the yes/no/maybe mix (yes 110, no 68, maybe 22). Each abstract becomes a PDF (`PUBMED_<pmid>`) with labelled sections. The long answer is kept as an alternative for the LLM judge.
- **CUAD:** the first 20 contracts of the test split × 10 categories: governing law, non-compete, termination for convenience, exclusivity, change of control, anti-assignment, cap on liability, uncapped liability, audit rights and insurance.
  - A category with a highlighted span becomes a `span` item, with the evidence pages located in the generated PDF.
  - One without becomes `absent` ("the contract has none"). Absent items test abstention, the failure that matters most in legal review.
- **Refusal:** 30 questions in six kinds (no such company, period not covered, out of domain, investment advice, personal data, false premise), written against the 84 FinanceBench filings.

## Personal data

None of the sources contain personal data about private individuals beyond what is published in PubMed abstracts and public contracts. The red-team corpus plants personal data on purpose, using only published test values:

- SSN 219-09-9999, invalid since the 1938 wallet-insert sample;
